# 06 — Leave worked examples

Each example should become an automated test. Unless stated, the leave year is **1 January – 31 December 2026**, and the 2026 bank holidays (England and Wales) are: **Thu 1 Jan, Fri 3 Apr, Mon 6 Apr, Mon 4 May, Mon 25 May, Mon 31 Aug, Fri 25 Dec, Mon 28 Dec** (Boxing Day substitute).

Answers are given in decimal hours and in hours and minutes (the display format, LEAVE-04). Rounding to 0.5 h applies only where a rule says so.

**Confidence:** *Agreed* = worked through and agreed in the interview. Examples LV-22 to LV-25 were added when writing and confirmed by the practice manager on review. *Unconfirmed* = depends on missing information.

## Allowances and day costs

### LV-01 — Full-time, whole year
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Staff group / contract | Any non-GP, 37.5 h/week |
| Rota | Mon–Fri, 7.5 h scheduled each day |
| Allowances typed in | Annual 172.5 h (23 days × 7.5); bank holiday 60 h (8 × 7.5) |
| Events | All 8 bank holidays added at 7.5 h; books Tue 3 Nov 2026 |
| Working | Bank holidays: 8 × 7.5 = 60 h → deficit/credit 0. 3 Nov costs 7.5 h |
| **Expected** | Annual leave **172.5 h (172 h 30 min)**; after 3 Nov **165 h** remaining |
| Rules | LEAVE-01, 05, 16, 17 |
| Confidence | Agreed |

### LV-02 — Part-time, equal days

## Starters, leavers and hours changes

### LV-06a — Starter
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Allowance | 210 h full-year (illustrative) |
| Events | Starts Mon 6 Jul 2026 |
| Working | 6 Jul–31 Dec = 179 days = 25 weeks + 4 days → **26 weeks**. 210 ÷ 52 × 26 = 105.00 |

| FindMyShift | Pro-rated by days: 210 × 179 ÷ 365 = 102.99 h |

| Confidence | Agreed |

### LV-07b — Leaver with leave over or remaining
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Contract | 37.5 h, Mon–Fri 7.5 h; annual 172.5 h, bank holiday 60 h |
| Events | Leaves Wed 14 Oct 2026 (41 weeks) |
| Working | Annual 172.5 ÷ 52 × 41 = 136.01 → **136.0**. Bank holiday 60 ÷ 52 × 41 = 47.31 → **47.5**. Bank holidays to 14 Oct: 1 Jan, 3 Apr, 6 Apr, 4 May, 25 May, 31 Aug = 6 × 7.5 = 45 h. Credit 47.5 − 45 = 2.5 |
| **Expected** | Total annual leave **138.5 h**. If 150 h taken: **11.5 h over**. If 120 h taken: **18.5 h remaining**. Shown on record and payroll report; managers flagged when date entered and 1 week before |

| Confidence | Agreed |
